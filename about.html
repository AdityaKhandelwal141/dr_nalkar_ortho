Practice overview

Dr. Abhijeet R Nalkar is an Orthopaedic Surgeon with qualifications MBBS, DNB (Ortho). Consultations are available at Saideep Sahyadri Hospital, Ahilyanagar and Nalkar Orthopedic Clinic, Rahuri.

The clinical focus includes robotic joint replacement, total knee and hip replacement using modern surgical approaches, unicondylar knee replacement, and knee and shoulder arthroscopy.

Treatment philosophy

Every treatment decision is made after individual clinical assessment. The information on this website is educational and is not a substitute for a consultation.

Whether any procedure is appropriate for you depends on your individual clinical assessment, imaging and overall health.

Areas of focus

  • Robotic Joint Replacement
  • Total Knee Replacement (TKA) — Subvastus Approach, Robotic-Assisted
  • Total Hip Replacement (THR) — Direct Anterior Approach (DAA), Robotic-Assisted
  • Unicondylar Knee Replacement (UKR)
  • Knee Scope (Knee Arthroscopy)
  • Shoulder Scope (Shoulder Arthroscopy)
