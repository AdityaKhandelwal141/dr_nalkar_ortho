Appointments & Consultations

You can request an appointment using the form on the Appointment page. The clinic can then contact you regarding availability.

Please bring any relevant medical records, recent imaging (X-rays, MRI) if available, a list of medications you take, and details of previous treatments.

Knee & Hip Replacement

The Subvastus approach is a surgical technique for knee replacement that accesses the joint while preserving the quadriceps tendon. Your surgeon will discuss whether it is suitable for your case.

It is a surgical technique that accesses the hip joint from the front. Suitability is determined by your surgeon based on clinical assessment.

UKR resurfaces only the affected compartment of the knee, while TKA addresses the joint more completely. The choice depends on the individual case.

Knee & Shoulder Arthroscopy

Knee arthroscopy is generally considered minimally invasive compared to open knee surgery. Your surgeon will discuss the specifics of your case.
