What is shoulder arthroscopy?

Shoulder arthroscopy (shoulder scope) is a minimally invasive procedure using a small camera to visualise the inside of the shoulder joint. It may be used for both diagnosis and treatment of certain shoulder conditions.

Why it may be considered

Your surgeon may recommend shoulder arthroscopy after clinical examination and imaging. Suitability depends on your individual condition.

Approach

Minimally Invasive

Small incisions are used to access the shoulder joint.

Purpose

Diagnostic & Therapeutic

Used for both evaluation and treatment of certain conditions.

Assessment

Surgeon-Led Evaluation

Clinical examination and imaging guide the decision.

Procedure overview

During shoulder arthroscopy, a small camera is inserted through a small incision. The surgeon can visualise the joint structures on a monitor and address certain conditions through additional small portals. The specifics of your procedure will be discussed with your surgeon.

Recovery considerations

Recovery varies depending on the procedure performed. Your surgeon will provide guidance and arrange follow-up based on your specific situation.
