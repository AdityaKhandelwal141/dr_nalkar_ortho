Technology

Robotic Joint
Replacement

Robotic-assisted joint replacement uses a pre-operative plan and robotic guidance to support the surgeon during bone preparation. It is a technology-led approach to knee and hip replacement.

Learn more Book Appointment

  • Planning

    A personalised plan is prepared from imaging before surgery.

  • Guidance

    The robotic system provides guidance based on that plan during the procedure.

  • Surgeon-led

    The surgeon remains in control and makes all clinical decisions.
